# VideoText brand mark

`VideoTextLogo.png` is the 1024x1024 RGBA master for the VideoText application icon. The mark combines video playback, transcript lines, and an audio waveform.

## Usage

- Keep the mark square and preserve its transparent outer area.
- Do not add text, outlines, shadows, or recolor individual elements.
- Use `AppIcon.icns` for the macOS application bundle.
- Regenerate `AppIcon.iconset` from the master when changing the artwork; do not upscale a smaller rendition.
- Keep enough clear space that the graphite silhouette remains distinct from surrounding content.

The production palette is graphite, white, coral red, and cyan. The icon is designed to remain identifiable at Finder and Dock sizes without a separate wordmark.
